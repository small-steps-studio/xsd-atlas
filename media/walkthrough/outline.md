## Outline and hover

The **Schema Outline** view (XSD Atlas icon in the activity bar) groups the file into imports and includes, elements, complex types, simple types, groups, attribute groups and attributes. Each entry shows its type or base type; expand it to see the sequence, choice and attributes inside. Click an entry to jump to it.

For a WSDL file the view lists the embedded schemas, messages, port types or interfaces with their operations, bindings with their SOAP actions, and services with their addresses.

Rest the pointer on a type or element name (in `type="..."`, `ref="..."` or `base="..."`) to see:

* where it is defined,
* its base types and effective facets,
* its child elements with occurrence, and its attributes,
* its documentation.

The same structure appears in the editor's Outline view and in the breadcrumbs.
