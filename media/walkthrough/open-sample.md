## Open a schema

XSD Atlas works with the files you already have: `.xsd` schemas and `.wsdl` service descriptions (WSDL 1.1 and 2.0), including schemas that import or include other files.

The sample commands open short, invented files in new editors:

* **Open Sample Schema**: a garden centre order with named types, an enumeration and a pattern.
* **Open Sample WSDL**: a small SOAP service with a request, a response and a fault.

Everything in this guide works on these samples.
