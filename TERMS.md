# Terms of purchase and use

Last updated: 2026-11-12. These terms summarise, in plain words, how XSD Atlas is offered. The binding text is the End User Licence Agreement in [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt); where the two differ, the agreement applies.

## What is free

Every feature that is not marked "Pro" is free: the Schema Outline, document symbols and breadcrumbs, hovers, go to definition, find references, the path at the cursor, the problem checks, Create Sample XML, the samples, the walkthrough and Report A Problem. The free features need no account, no key and no network connection, and they will not be moved behind the licence key in a later version.

## The trial

The Pro features (schema comparison and its reports, Create Full Sample XML, schema documentation, Flatten Schema, SOAP envelope samples, the workspace inventory) can be tried free of charge in a 14-day trial per computer. The trial starts the first time you run a Pro command, not when you install the extension. It is recorded only in the editor's storage on that computer and is not renewed. **XSD Atlas: Show Licence Status** shows how many days are left.

## The Pro licences

* **Pro Personal**: USD 29, one-time, at the time of writing. For one person who pays for it; use at work is allowed.
* **Pro Business**: USD 59 per user, one-time, at the time of writing. For companies and other organisations: one key per person who uses Pro (a purchase for N users gives N keys). The organisation may move a key from one of its users to another.
* **Computers.** Each key may be active on up to three computers of its user at the same time.
* **Duration.** Keys do not expire and cover every update published as XSD Atlas.
* **Moving.** Run **XSD Atlas: Remove Licence From This Machine** on the old computer, then enter the key on the new one. If the old computer is gone, contact support so the activation can be released.
* **Not allowed.** Sharing, publishing, reselling or transferring a key (other than moving a Business key within the organisation); one key for several people; bypassing the licence check or the trial.

The checkout page shows the final price and the taxes for your country before you pay. There is no subscription and no renewal fee.

## Payment, invoices and refunds

Licence keys are sold through Dodo Payments, the payment provider and **merchant of record**. The purchase contract for the key is between you and the merchant of record; its buyer terms apply to payment, invoices and refunds, and refund requests are handled by it under those terms. The author of XSD Atlas does not process payments and never sees card or bank details. The key is e-mailed to the address you enter at checkout right after the payment.

## Data

XSD Atlas sends none of your files or file names anywhere and has no telemetry. The only network request is the licence check with the payment provider after you enter a key; the trial uses no network at all. Details are in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

Its checks, comparisons and classifications are aids, not a replacement for testing with your partners' systems, and it does not validate XML documents against a schema. The author's liability is limited to the price you paid for the key (zero for the free features and the trial), as far as the law allows; mandatory consumer rights are not affected.

## Support

Questions, bug reports and licence problems: <https://github.com/small-steps-studio/xsd-atlas/issues>. In the editor, **XSD Atlas: Report A Problem** copies a diagnostics block (no file contents, no key) and opens that page. Never post a licence key or confidential data in a public issue.
